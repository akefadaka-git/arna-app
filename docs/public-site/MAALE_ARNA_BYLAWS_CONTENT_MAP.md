## 6. Corporate narrative — publication treatment

The Owner supplied the following additional subjects for potential corporate pages. Apply the publication safeguards below:

- **Leadership and governance:** Describe the Board of Directors' strategic direction and oversight only at a general level. Do not publish named directors, biographies, qualifications or claims about current board composition until verified against current company records and approved for publication.
- **Community and social responsibility:** Public-facing content should focus on constructive, inclusive corporate responsibility priorities: education and skills development; community health and well-being; environmental care; and local economic opportunity, enterprise and sustainable livelihoods. The approach should be needs-led, locally relevant, proportionate to the Company's capacity, based on approved plans and partnerships, and reported transparently.
- **Religious and individual member conduct:** The Owner has decided that the references in the supplied narrative to Christianity, individual members' tithing and church participation should not be included in the public corporate responsibility statement. These are not to be represented as corporate CSR commitments or obligations of the Company.
- **Evidence and reporting:** The website must distinguish priorities and intentions from funded programs, active partnerships, delivered projects and measurable outcomes. Do not imply that a program exists, has been funded or has delivered results unless evidence is reviewed and the specific claim is approved. Do not promise recurring financial contributions.
- **Financial performance:** The supplied narrative claims strong historical financial performance, continuous growth and sustainable profitability. Treat these as **not publishable pending evidence** such as approved/audited financial statements, reporting period, accounting basis and explicit authorization. General strategic ambitions may be published without asserting financial results.
- **Company history:** Owner states that the company was established in **2016 E.C.** with **29 founding shareholders**. Confirm exact incorporation/registration date and founding shareholder count against the registration certificate, founding minutes or authoritative company register before publishing as verified corporate history. Do not expose shareholder personal data.

### Permanent Community Responsibility section — Owner decision (2026-11-14)

The public corporate website must retain **Community Responsibility** as a permanent institutional section. Its continued presence must not depend on whether the Company currently has a project to announce. The section may describe enduring corporate principles and priority areas, including education and skills development, community health and well-being, environmental care, and local economic opportunity.

Specific project records are a separate, evidence-controlled publication layer. Publish a named project, budget or expenditure, funding commitment, partner, activity, beneficiary figure, deliverable or outcome only when:
1. the relevant information is supported by current, reviewable company documentation;
2. the appropriate company authority has approved the specific information for public release; and
3. the published statement accurately identifies its status, period, scope and evidence (for example, planned, approved, funded, in progress or completed).

Do not turn a priority area into a claim of an active program. Do not publish unapproved budgets, partner names or logos, personal/beneficiary information, impact figures or outcome claims. If there are no approved project updates, keep the permanent section visible and show only the approved corporate-level priorities; do not remove or hide the section and do not fabricate activity.
